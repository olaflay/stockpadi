## 2. Phase 2 Features (What Pro Users Get & Why They Pay)

Phase 2 turns StockPadi from a simple sales tool into an **all-in-one business guard** that stops staff theft and tracks real profit.

| Feature | What It Does (In Simple Terms) | Why the Shop Owner Will Pay for It |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Multi-Branch Control** | Owner sees all 3 shops from their phone without traveling there. | Stops branch managers from hiding daily sales. |
| **2. Cashier vs Owner Logins** | Cashiers can only sell; they cannot see profit margins or edit past sales. | Stops cashiers from tampering with prices or stealing cash. |
| **3. Real Profit & Loss Report** | Automatically calculates: *Sales − Buying Cost − Shop Expenses = Real Take-Home Profit*. | Shop owners finally know if they are making money or losing money. |
| **4. Anti-Theft Stock Audit Trail** | Every time stock changes, the app records who changed it, the exact time, and why. | Catches staff who quietly remove goods from the shelf. |
| **5. Low Stock & Expiry Alerts** | Warns the owner before popular goods finish or before drugs/food expire. | Prevents lost sales and throwing away expired stock. |
| **6. 1-Tap Customer Debt Reminders** | Sends automatic polite debt reminder messages to customers over WhatsApp. | Recovers money owed to the shop much faster. |
| **7. Excel / CSV Data Export** | 1-click download of all sales and expenses for taxes or bank records. | Saves days of manual bookkeeping. |

## 3. Phase 3 Features (What Enterprise Users Get at Scale)

Phase 3 is for bigger retail supermarkets, pharmacies, and merchants with 4 to 6 stores.

| Feature | What It Does (In Simple Terms) | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Inter-Branch Stock Transfers** | Move 20 cartons from Branch 1 to Branch 2 with a digital transfer waybill. | Prevents goods from getting lost between store locations. |
| **2. Custom Receipt Branding** | Puts the store's full logo, address, and promo message on printed and WhatsApp receipts. | Makes the shop look big, professional, and trusted. |
| **3. Reorder & Supplier Management** | Generates purchase orders for suppliers when stock drops below minimum levels. | Automates restocking from distributors. |
| **4. Customer Loyalty & Points** | Customers earn points when they buy, encouraging them to return. | Keeps customers coming back to this shop instead of competitors. |
| **5. Working Capital Loan Link** | Uses the shop's verified sales history to help them get fast inventory loans from bank partners. | StockPadi earns a referral commission (1.5% - 2.5%) on every loan. |
